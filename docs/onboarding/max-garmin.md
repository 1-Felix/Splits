# Max's Forerunner 265S: setup checklist

Your SPLITS dashboard now reads straight from Garmin instead of the phone
bridge app. A few settings on the watch and in Garmin Connect make sure the
numbers on the watch, in Garmin and in SPLITS all agree. It takes about 10
minutes. (Menu names may differ slightly in your version of the Garmin Connect
app.)

---

## 1. Your profile and heart-rate zones

In the Garmin Connect app, open your profile / user settings and check:

- **Birth date**: set so it shows age **23**.
- **Max heart rate**: **199**. This is your measured value, so enter it
  yourself instead of letting Garmin guess from your age.
- **Resting heart rate**: leave on **automatic**.
- **Heart rate zones**: (app -> your profile -> Heart Rate Zones) set
  **Based on: BPM** (custom zones), then type in these **exact zone edges**.
  Z5 runs up to your max HR of 199. Zones based on resting heart rate would
  shift on their own whenever your resting HR changes; fixed bpm edges keep the
  watch and SPLITS reading the same numbers.

| Zone | Low edge (bpm) |
|------|----------------|
| Z1   | 126 |
| Z2   | 140 |
| Z3   | 155 |
| Z4   | 170 |
| Z5   | 184 |

These come from max HR 199 and a resting HR of 52 (your Garmin 7-day average
on 2026-10-21). If SPLITS' resting HR is changed later, this table changes with
it.

## 2. Uninstall SPLITS Bridge

The old bridge app on your Pixel is no longer needed. Uninstall it. Garmin
Connect's Health Connect sharing can stay on; it does no harm.

## 3. Wear the watch overnight

Sleep and HRV (heart-rate variability) come from the night. HRV status needs
about 3 weeks of nights to build your baseline, so the readiness and HRV cards
stay thin until then. That is normal.

## 4. Record strength and mobility on the watch

Start a **Strength** activity on the watch for those sessions. SPLITS can only
score those plan days if it sees them. Once two are recorded within 90 days,
they start counting. Until then they show as "untracked", not "missed".

## 5. Ignore the early race predictor

Garmin's race predictor is optimistic for new users. SPLITS shows it, but your
plan does not trust it. The plan re-anchors on your **Wk 16 5K on 2026-11-07**,
and that run is the real number.
